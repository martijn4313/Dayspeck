Clothing

The outfits of the picture screens, by temperature (°C, always metric). Each limit must be equal to or below the one above it. The wind limit also blows the weather report away.

sunny daytime only
t-shirt above
gusts, dry weather
dinner only: its temperature, and sun, sunset or moon

Countdowns

The countdown screen counts the sleeps to a birthday or holiday when it is near (put it in a list under Screens). The cake has a candle for every year and the letter on it.

no date = none
